<p>In addition to customer-facing experiences, I worked on the frontend and UX of the NextCommerce platform itself—used by marketers and operators to manage products, campaigns, and customer data.</p>

<p>Within the NextCommerce platform UI, the challenge was translating complex ecommerce functionality into interfaces that are both powerful and usable.</p>

<p>My contributions included:</p>

<ul>
  <li>UI design and frontend implementation of key product surfaces</li>
  <li>Improving usability of campaign configuration and product workflows</li>
  <li>Creating clearer mental models for complex operations</li>
  <li>Aligning platform UI with frontend experience patterns</li>
</ul>

<p>This required balancing technical constraints with usability—ensuring the platform remains flexible without becoming difficult to navigate.</p>
